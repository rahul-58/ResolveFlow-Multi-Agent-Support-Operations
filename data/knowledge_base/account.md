# Account access

## Password reset email missing

Ask the customer to check spam or junk mail and confirm that they are using the
email address associated with the account. The support team may resend a reset
email after verifying the account through the approved support process.

Never ask for a password, one-time authentication code, or full payment details.
If the customer still cannot access the account after the approved reset flow,
escalate the ticket to account support.
