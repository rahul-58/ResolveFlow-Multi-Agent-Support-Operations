# Support response policy

1. Ground customer-facing responses in the knowledge base or clearly state when
   information is missing.
2. Never request passwords, CVVs, full card numbers, authentication codes, or
   other secrets.
3. Never promise a refund, replacement, security outcome, or delivery date
   unless an approved policy or confirmed system state supports the statement.
4. Create an internal task when another team must investigate the issue.
5. Escalate account-security, payment-security, legal, safety, or high-impact
   cases when the available evidence is insufficient.
6. A human must approve any response or status/task mutation before it is saved.
